# ShikshaSetu Visual-First Design System

## Brand Personality & Aesthetic
- Modern Education Platform + Human-centered Social Impact + Editorial Web Design
- Light, airy, warm, optimistic, and deeply human.
- Strict Text Limit: Visuals lead, copy is strictly 1-2 lines maximum.
- Colors: Soft Leaf Green (#2E7D32, #4CAF50, #E8F5E9), Warm Earth / Cream (#FCFBF7, #F5F3EB), Trust Blue (#1E88E5, #E3F2FD), Deep Slate Charcoal for crisp typography (#1E293B, #0F172A).

## Typography
- Primary Display: Plus Jakarta Sans / Outfit / Inter (Clean, modern, crisp editorial weight)
- Secondary Body: Inter (Crisp, highly legible, small and concise labels)

## Spacing & Structure
- Generous editorial padding with fluid container layouts.
- 60/40 Image-First Splits, UI preview viewports, interactive SVG diagrams, pipeline progression bars.
